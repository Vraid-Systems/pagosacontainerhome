Crew stays

Working a job around Pagosa? This is a house, not a motel room, on an acre west of town with room for the trucks.

What your crew gets

Parking

A gravel pad with room for up to six vehicles. Trailers welcome.

Beds

Two bedrooms with a king in each, plus a futon in the main room. Two workers each get their own room and a third takes the futon. A crew of four shares.

After the shift

A washer and dryer for work clothes, and a full kitchen so you're not eating out every night.

Internet

100 Mbps down and 10 Mbps up. Enough for bids, plans and video calls home.

Early starts

Self check-in with a door code. Leaving before dawn is fine. Please don't idle trucks before 7 am; the neighbors are close enough to hear.

EV charging

A Level 2 charger on site, so an electric work truck starts every morning full. Coming winter 2026-27.

Supplies

Terry's Ace Hardware, City Market and fuel are uptown, on the way into town. Durango is an hour west.

Dogs

Up to two. Add them to the reservation.

Good fit for

  • Builders and subcontractors working new homes around Aspen Springs and Pagosa
  • Container and modular home delivery and set crews
  • Utility, line and broadband crews
  • Wildfire mitigation, forestry and Forest Service contract crews
  • Agency and company staff on work travel

Stays and booking

Stays run 2 to 14 nights, with a weekly discount. Book through Airbnb for itemized receipts that work for expense reports.

Ground rules

  • Up to 4 guests, set by the county permit.
  • Parking is for the vehicles you're driving. No staging materials or equipment for other job sites, and no work on the property.
  • Keep vehicles on the gravel pad.
  • No smoking, inside or out.
  • Quiet hours 10 pm to 7 am.
  • Guests must be 21 or older to book.

The road up is dirt and gravel. Work trucks handle it fine, but November through April bring good tires and check Wolf Creek Pass conditions before you drive in from the east.

Talk to us first

Questions about dates, crew size, billing or trailer length before you book? Email lodging@pagosacontainerhome.com and you'll hear back from the owner.
