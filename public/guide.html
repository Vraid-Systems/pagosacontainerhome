Arriving

  • Self check-in. Your door code comes in your booking messages. It works only during your stay.
  • Address. The exact address is in your booking, not on this website.
  • Parking. Park on the gravel pad. There is room for up to six vehicles, and trailers are welcome.
  • Check-in and check-out times are in your booking confirmation.

House rules

  • No smoking, inside or out.
  • Up to two dogs. No other pets.
  • Guests must be 21 or older to book.

Drinking water

RULEDrink and cook with water from the small chrome tap at the kitchen sink.

Water from the main faucet is safe, but it tastes foul. The reverse osmosis tap filters it, so use that one for drinking water, coffee and cooking. The main faucet is fine for washing.

Heat & AC

RULEUse the mini-splits to heat and cool the house. Leave the heat bars on 62°F.

Mini-splits: use these

There are three mini-splits: one in each bedroom and one in the main room. They change the temperature faster and more efficiently than the heat bars. Use the remote to set the mode and temperature.

Keep all three on the same modeSet every mini-split to heat, or every one to cool. They won't run with some on heat and some on cool.
Give them 5 minutesAfter switching between heat and cool, they can take about five minutes to start blowing warm or cool air. That's normal.

Heat bars: leave on 62°F

The long white bars near the ceiling are electric resistance heaters. They're slower to change the temperature than the mini-splits, and they use a lot more power.

Their wall thermostats are set to 62°F. Please leave them there. If you do change one, set it back to 62°F before you leave.

Kitchen sink

RULEKeep the strainer in the kitchen sink drain. Empty it into the trash, not down the drain.

The house is on a septic system. The strainer catches food scraps that would otherwise clog the septic, make it back up into the house, or make it smell. Grease and oil go in the trash too.

Trash

If the kitchen trash fills up, there's an additional large trash can in the pumphouse: the small metal building to the right of the house as you face it from the driveway.

Check-out

Check-out time is in your booking. Before you go:

Tap items to check them off. Nothing is saved or sent.

Thanks for staying. If anything broke or ran out, tell us in your booking messages so we can fix it before the next guest.
